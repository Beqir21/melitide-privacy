Melitide Privacy Policy

This policy applies to the Melitide Android app (com.melitide.app) distributed through Google Play by Skicora. It takes effect when the app is first distributed to users.

What the app uses

Melitide stores the period dates, flow, symptoms, mood, spotting, and estimated pregnancy due date that you enter. Daily check-ins may also include weight, sleep, water intake, physical activity, sexual activity and sex drive, and home ovulation test results. Melitide uses these entries to show your history, estimate future period dates when enough information is available, and display your pregnancy week from your entered due date. It saves event details, packing lists, reminders, and preferences to support your plans.

Where records stay

Records are encrypted in Android’s private app storage using a key managed by Android Keystore. Melitide has no account, server, ads, analytics, or cloud sync. It does not send your records to the developer, sell them, or share them with advertisers. Melitide excludes its records from Android backup and device transfer.

Exports

When you ask Melitide to export your records, Android lets you choose where to save the readable JSON file. The file includes the records and preferences you have entered. The destination you choose may be managed by another app or a cloud provider. Keep exported records somewhere you trust and delete any copies you no longer need.

External health guides

When you choose to open a health guide, the page opens in your browser and follows the website’s own privacy policy. Melitide does not attach your records to guide links.

Notifications

When you turn on notifications and schedule a reminder, Melitide asks Android to deliver a generic notification. The notification omits your event title and health information. You can turn notifications off in Android settings.

Retention and deletion

Melitide keeps records on your device until you edit or delete them, clear the app’s data, or uninstall it. In Melitide, open Settings, then Privacy & data, and choose Clear app records to remove all local records and cancel reminders. Deleting records in the app leaves files you previously exported in the locations where you saved them.
